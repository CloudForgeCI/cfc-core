# Sponsor CloudForgeCI

CloudForgeCI is an open source project that makes deploying production-ready Jenkins environments on AWS fast, secure, and repeatable. If you or your company use CloudForgeCI, please consider sponsoring its development.

## Why Sponsor?

**If you're using CloudForgeCI, you're already saving thousands of dollars:**

- Manual Jenkins setup on AWS: 80-200 hours of DevOps engineering time ($6,000-$15,000)
- Ongoing maintenance: 5-10 hours/month ($400-$800/month)
- **CloudForgeCI setup time: 30 minutes**

Your sponsorship helps ensure the project remains actively maintained, secure, and continues to evolve with new features.

## Where Your Sponsorship Goes

- Active maintenance and bug fixes
- Security updates and dependency management
- New feature development for the community edition
- Comprehensive documentation and examples
- Community support and engagement
- Infrastructure costs (CI/CD, testing, hosting)
